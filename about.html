About OM Juice & Shakes

How a quiet obsession became a craft juice & shake counter in Rewari.

Our Family Story

OM Juice & Shakes was founded by Omprakash Saini and Bhagwan Dass Saini — two brothers who believed that a glass of juice made with love could brighten anyone's day.

What started as a small juice counter grew into a Rewari favourite, thanks to their dedication to fresh fruit, honest ingredients, and never cutting corners. Every squeeze, every shake, every serving carried their warmth — and the people of Rewari felt it.

Today, we're just as loved for our sweets & snacks as we are for our juices. From hot, crispy samosas, pav bhaji and dosa made fresh at our snack counter, to the Sweets Corner with soft rasgullas, rich gulab jamun, melt-in-mouth motichoor laddo, desi ghee gajarpak and creamy ras malai — every item is made with the same honest ingredients and the same warmth. Nothing is pre-made, nothing sits in a tray all day; every sweet and snack is prepared fresh so it reaches you the way it should — fresh, flavourful, and full of the love Rewari has come to expect from us.

Today, their children — Sumit Saini, Bharat Saini and brothers Deepanshu Saini, Rajat Saini — run OM Juice & Shakes with the same values they taught us — quality first, hospitality always — and with their blessings guiding every glass we serve, every bite we make. We're proud to carry their legacy forward, one juice, one snack, and one sweet at a time.
